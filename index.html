      Questions, answered.

      Do I need to be at C1 already?

      No. The course is designed for learners with a solid B2 foundation.

      Must I finish in 90 days?

      No. Ninety days is the intensive goal, not a deadline.

      Can I split a lesson across several days?

      Yes. Your work and progress are saved.

      Is the whole course in English?

      Yes. MIYU Tutor can explain a selected part in Russian when asked.

      Will this give me an official C1 certificate?

      No. Course completion is not the same as a Cambridge or other official language qualification.

      How much does it cost?

      Pricing and access details will be announced before launch.

      Does speaking practice happen on this site?

      Interactive speaking is planned; availability at launch will be confirmed.
